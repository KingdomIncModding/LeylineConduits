[b]Leyline Conduits[/b]
Link distant machines with small enchanted conduits. Give an Input and an Output the same leyline name to move items between them, including across workshop tabs.

[b]Progression and costs[/b]
Research Portal first, then purchase Leyline Conduits under Tech Tree → Mods. Build conduits from the Utility category.

Leyline research costs four times Portal's research price in Gold and Mana, including research discounts. Each conduit costs twice Portal's construction price in Gold and Rings. Transfers cost 12 Mana per item, reduced to 6 and then 3 by Portal cost-reduction research. Mana is charged once on a successful transfer. Insufficient Mana leaves the item waiting; blocked, disconnected and incompatible destinations consume no Mana.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Small 64 x 64 tiles, roughly the size of a one-cell sticker.[/*]
[*]One connection per conduit: use its gear to choose Input or Output and enter a leyline name.[/*]
[*]A tiny top grip lets you drag the conduit or click to pick it up. Right-click cancels placement.[/*]
[*]Named channels connect matching conduits across all workshop tabs in the current kingdom.[/*]
[*]Set Icon opens a searchable picker of game icons. Rune restores the default bronze and blue crystal seal.[/*]
[*]Hover to see the mode, leyline name, status and current Mana rate.[/*]
[*]Multiple Outputs receive an Input's items in round-robin order, skipping disconnected, occupied or incompatible destinations.[/*]
[*]Each conduit holds one item. Equipment and adventurer details stay with the item.[/*]
[*]Modes, names, icons, pipes and waiting items persist in saves. Copies retain settings without duplicating inventory.[/*]
[/list]

[b]Requirements[/b]
Kingdom Inc. for Windows, BepInEx 5 and Foundation 0.5.0 or later, installed separately.

[b]Installation[/b]
1. Close the game and install the requirements.
2. Extract the Leyline Conduits player ZIP into the folder containing KingdomInc.exe.
3. The plugin should be at BepInEx/plugins/LeylineConduits/LeylineConduits.dll.
4. Launch the game. Research Portal, then Leyline Conduits under Tech Tree → Mods.

When updating, replace the existing DLL and avoid duplicate copies.

[b]How to use[/b]
1. Place a conduit, open its gear, choose Input, enter a name such as iron, and Apply.
2. Connect your producer's output to the conduit's green input.
3. Place another conduit, choose Output, use the same name, and Apply.
4. Connect the blue output to your destination. The matching conduit can be on another workshop tab.
5. Use Set Icon in the gear menu to customize either conduit, then Apply.

Names ignore capitalization and leading or trailing spaces, with a maximum of 32 characters. Unnamed Inputs accept nothing. Opening settings pauses that conduit's transfers; close settings to resume. Disconnect the pipe before switching mode, and drain waiting items before changing the mode or name. Icons can be changed while items are waiting or pipes are connected. Closing settings without Apply cancels your changes.

[b]Saves and removal[/b]
Existing purchased research, names, modes, icons, pipes and waiting items are retained when updating. Missing or unavailable saved icons use the default rune.

Keep Leyline Conduits installed while loading saves containing conduits. Before removing it, let waiting items reach their destinations, remove every conduit from every workshop tab, save and close the game. Then remove BepInEx/plugins/LeylineConduits.
